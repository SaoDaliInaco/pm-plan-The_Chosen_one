# DR-01 · Open-day demo scope, week 10

- **Status:** Approved (2026-10-24)
- **Requested by:** Head of the IT department (Sponsor)
- **Context:** Sponsor requested all Should features to be completed by week 10 for a departmental open day. Capacity calculations show a 22-hour shortfall if all Should features are pulled forward.

## Options Considered
1. **Full Should scope by W10:** Violated capacity constraints (262h needed vs 240h available).
2. **Counter-proposal 1 (Selected):** Complete Must features plus F09 (Responsive Design) and F10 (Analytics) by week 10 (202h total), retaining a 38-hour safety buffer. F11, F12, and F13 remain at week 14.
3. **Counter-proposal 2:** Mocked analytics demo build (8h) for week 10, pushing all Should features to week 14.

## Decision
Counter-proposal 1 is approved. F09 and F10 move forward to the week-10 open-day milestone.

## Consequences
- **Schedule Baseline:** A new milestone ("Open-day demo") is added at week 10.
- **Risks Added:** Risk R-10 ("Demonstrating analytics on a compressed schedule") is added to the risk register.
- **Won't / Deferred Scope:** F11 (Export), F12 (Notifications), and F13 (Multimedia) are officially deferred to the week-14 pilot release.
